Privacy Policy

2 Information We Collect & How It Is Used

A. Local Learning & Progress Data (On-Device Storage)

The application stores educational progress locally on your device within an encrypted/sandboxed SQLite database (vce_vocab.db). This includes:

  • Current Day in the 365-Day senior English roadmap and highest unlocked day
  • Daily completion states across Daily Vocab Cards, Verbal Reasoning Drills, and Reading Challenges
  • Flashcard review intervals and SM-2 spaced-repetition memory weights
  • Daily 10-question vocab quiz selections and saved test answers
  • Weekly 20-item milestone assessment scores and completion timestamps
  • Consecutive day streak counts and milestone badges

Purpose: To provide offline progression, resume active quiz sessions, and prevent data loss during daily practice. This data remains on your physical device and is never uploaded to our servers or sold to third parties.

B. On-Device AI Sentence Evaluator

When you type analytical sentences into the AI Sentence Evaluator:

  • Sentence evaluation algorithms run locally / on-device to check grammatical syntax, academic register, part-of-speech accuracy, and vocabulary integration.
  • Your input sentences are not logged on external servers and are not harvested to train public foundational AI models.

C. Device & Diagnostic Information

Like most mobile software, the app may automatically collect non-identifiable technical diagnostics via operating system frameworks, including:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version (e.g. iOS 18, Android 14)
  • Language and locale settings (e.g. en_AU)
  • Hardware capabilities (RAM and GPU availability check to ensure sentence evaluation compatibility)
  • Anonymous crash logs and execution latency metrics to troubleshoot stability

3 Third-Party SDKs and Integrations

To support free access to Australian secondary educational content and ensure seamless performance, the app integrates select industry-standard third-party SDKs:

Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)

The app displays banner and rewarded video ads through Google AdMob (Publisher ID: ca-app-pub-7669371125149782). AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers such as:

  • Mobile advertising identifiers (Apple IDFA or Google Advertising ID / GAID), subject to your explicit operating system tracking permission
  • IP address (used to infer broad general geographical region, e.g., Australia)
  • Ad interaction statistics and diagnostics for fraud prevention

We implement the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to provide transparent European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and global consent management. Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time via device settings or in-app ad consent prompts. Review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The Conversation (AU) Content Attribution Pixel

Curated educational articles included in the Reading Challenge module from The Conversation (AU) are republished under Creative Commons (CC BY-ND 4.0). Under their republishing terms, our app loads a 1x1 measurement beacon (counter.theconversation.com) to register article readership. This pixel does not collect personally identifiable user information. Review their republishing guidelines at https://theconversation.com/au/republishing-guidelines.

In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Apple StoreKit & Google Play Billing)

If you choose to upgrade to Aussie English Pro, all payment transactions are handled directly through Apple's App Store or Google Play Store in-app billing systems. AcornWork Digital Consulting Australia Pty Ltd never receives, processes, or stores your credit card number, bank details, or billing address.

5 Children's Privacy Protection (COPPA & Australian Safety Standards)

Our educational apps are designed for secondary and senior high school students (Years 7–12; ages 12 and above) preparing for Australian Year 11/12 certifications (VCE, HSC, QCE, SACE, WACE).

We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 without verifiable parental or school consent. If you believe that a child under 13 has inadvertently provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately at info@acornwise.com.au, and we will take prompt measures to delete such records.

6 Data Retention, Reset, and Account Deletion

Because our application operates on an offline-first, local SQLite architecture without requiring an external user account:

  • Local Data Retention: Your completed days, streaks, flashcard intervals, and quiz history are retained only in local storage on your device for as long as the application remains installed.
  • Complete Data Reset / Deletion: You can completely purge all saved progress, scores, and cached data at any time by:
    1. Selecting Clear App Data / Storage in your device's operating system settings (Android), or
    2. Deleting/uninstalling the application from your device (iOS and Android).

Uninstalling the app immediately removes all local SQLite databases and local settings from your device.
